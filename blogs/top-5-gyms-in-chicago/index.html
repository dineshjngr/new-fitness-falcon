Top 5 Gyms in Chicago

By Dinesh September 30, 2026 4 min read 0 Comments

Chicago, the city of broad shoulders and big dreams, is also home to a vibrant fitness scene. Whether you're a seasoned gym rat or a newbie looking to lace up your sneakers for the first time, there's a perfect workout haven waiting for you in this dynamic metropolis. But with so many options, where do you even begin?

Fear not, fitness enthusiasts! We've compiled a list of the top 6 gyms in Chicago, catering to diverse needs and budgets. So, grab your water bottle, crank up your workout playlist, and get ready to break a sweat!

1. Mode Gym:

Website: https://getinmode.com/

Google Maps Rating: 4.7 stars (over 1,800 reviews)

Contract Details: Various membership options available, including month-to-month, 3-month, 6-month, and annual plans. Day passes also offered.

Why We Love It: Mode Gym is a sleek and stylish haven for fitness enthusiasts seeking a luxurious workout experience. Think top-of-the-line equipment, a spacious and airy atmosphere, and a passionate community that motivates and inspires. From state-of-the-art cardio machines and free weights to dedicated studios for group fitness classes like spin and yoga, Mode Gym has everything you need to reach your fitness goals.

2. The Garage Chicago Gym:

Website: http://www.thegaragechi.com/

Google Maps Rating: 5 stars (over 250 reviews)

Contract Details: Month-to-month memberships and personal training packages available.

Why We Love It: The Garage isn't your typical gym. It's a haven for those who crave a more raw, functional training experience. Think heavy tires, ropes, climbing walls, and open spaces that encourage creativity and pushing your limits. The Garage fosters a strong sense of community, with experienced trainers who guide and motivate you to conquer your fitness challenges.

3. Number One Gym:

Website: http://numberonegym.com/

Google Maps Rating: 4.5 stars (over 1,500 reviews)

Contract Details: Multiple membership options available, including individual and family plans. Day passes also offered.

Why We Love It: Number One Gym lives up to its name, offering a comprehensive fitness experience for everyone. Whether you're a powerlifter, a cardio enthusiast, or simply looking to tone up, this gym has the equipment and classes to help you achieve your goals. The friendly and knowledgeable staff is always on hand to provide guidance and support, making Number One Gym a welcoming space for all fitness levels.

4. Chicago Fitness Center:

Website- https://www.chicagofitnesscenter.com/

Google Maps Rating: 4.8 stars (over 1,200 reviews)

Contract Details: Month-to-month and annual memberships available. Corporate discounts offered.

Why We Love It: Chicago Fitness Center is a 24/7 gym that caters to busy schedules and diverse workout preferences. With a vast selection of cardio and strength training equipment, dedicated workout areas for CrossFit and Olympic weightlifting, and a plethora of group fitness classes, this gym keeps things interesting and challenging. The convenient location and extended hours make it a perfect fit for those who value flexibility and accessibility.

5. Anytime Fitness:

Website- https://www.anytimefitness.com/gyms/3918/chicago-il-60647/

Google Maps Rating: 4.8 stars (over 700 reviews)

Contract Details: Various membership options available, including individual and family plans. 24/7 access included with all memberships.

Why We Love It: Convenience meets affordability at Anytime Fitness. With multiple locations across the city, finding a gym near you is a breeze. The 24/7 access allows you to work out on your own schedule, and the friendly staff is always happy to help you navigate the equipment and classes. Whether you're a seasoned gym-goer or just starting out, Anytime Fitness offers a welcoming and supportive environment to reach your fitness goals.

Bonus Tip: Before committing to a gym, take advantage of free trials or day passes to experience the atmosphere and see if it's the right fit for you. Consider your budget, location, and fitness goals when making your decision. And most importantly, have fun and enjoy the journey!

Remember, Chicago is your fitness playground. So grab your gym bag, lace up your shoes, and get ready to conquer your fitness goals in the Windy City! Read more: Exploring the Top 10 Fitness Gyms in the USA
