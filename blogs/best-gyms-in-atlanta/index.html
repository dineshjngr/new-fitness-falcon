The best gym in Atlanta depends on what you want to pay and where you'll actually show up. For a cheap, no-contract strength gym with long hours, Atlanta Barbell in Decatur is $90 a month or $20 for a day. For a well-equipped independent gym in town, Urban Body Fitness is $62.99 a month with free parking. For a resort-style club, Life Time Buckhead at Phipps Plaza starts at $299. For pools, courts and childcare on a budget, the Carl E. Sanders Family YMCA at Buckhead is $79 a month for adults and $49 for ages 18 to 29. For coached classes, CrossFit Buckhead and CrossFit Midtown both take beginners, and Workout Anytime on Howell Mill Road is the 24/7 option we could verify.

Key takeaways

  • Atlanta Barbell in Decatur is the best-value strength gym we verified: $90 a month with no contract or setup fee, 5am–11pm every day, and a $20 day pass.
  • Urban Body Fitness is $62.99 a month with free parking and towel service, but it moves to 812 Lambert Drive in early 2027.
  • Life Time Buckhead at Phipps Plaza starts at $299 a month with two pools and a spa; the Buckhead YMCA is $79 for adults and $49 for ages 18–29.
  • Workout Anytime on Howell Mill Road is the only verified 24/7 gym on the list, but it doesn't publish prices.

How this list was made: The Fitness Falcon did not visit, tour or train at any of these gyms. This is a research-based guide. Every gym was checked on its official website and at least one independent listing between 12 and 18 October 2026. No gym paid to be included. Prices are exactly as each gym publishes them, and where a gym doesn't publish a price we say so. If you're weighing a national chain instead, our guide to US gym chain membership prices and fees compares the big names, including the budget chains we couldn't verify locally.

What gym memberships cost in Atlanta

Among the gyms we could verify, published monthly prices run from $49 (YMCA, ages 18 to 29) to $299 (Life Time Buckhead). The headline number is only part of the cost:

  • Joining and setup fees. The Y charges $39 to $59 to join. Atlanta Barbell charges $0. Urban Body Fitness lists no enrollment fee. Life Time, CrossFit Buckhead and Workout Anytime don't publish one on the pages we checked.
  • Commitment terms. CrossFit Midtown requires 12 months plus a $298 Foundation package. Atlanta Barbell and Life Time are cancel-anytime or month-to-month. Ask the others.
  • Experience requirements. Both CrossFit boxes only sell drop-ins to experienced athletes. Beginners go through the free intro class at Buckhead or the Foundation package at Midtown.
  • Day passes. Atlanta Barbell's $20 pass is the cheapest published single visit. Life Time and the Y sell day passes without publishing the price. Urban Body Fitness has a $25 week-long trial instead.

National budget chains may be cheaper. Planet Fitness, Crunch, Anytime Fitness and EōS all have Atlanta-area clubs, but none could be verified from their official pages for this guide. Our US gym chain price comparison covers their published rates and annual fees.

How to choose a gym in Atlanta

  1. Start with your schedule. Only Workout Anytime on this list is open around the clock. Life Time opens at 4am on weekdays and Atlanta Barbell runs 5am–11pm every day, which covers most early and late trainers without 24/7 access. The CrossFit boxes and the Y close early on weekends.
  2. Pick a neighborhood you'll pass anyway. The picks cover Buckhead and Phipps (Life Time), Miami Circle (CrossFit Buckhead), Moores Mill (the Y), Howell Mill (Workout Anytime), Midtown and Amsterdam Walk (CrossFit Midtown, Urban Body Fitness) and Decatur (Atlanta Barbell). A gym on your commute is easier to stick with than a better one across town. That's general advice, not a measured finding.
  3. Check parking. Urban Body Fitness includes free parking. CrossFit Buckhead publishes where to park. Life Time is inside Phipps Plaza, and we couldn't verify its parking terms. For transit, use MARTA's trip planner rather than assuming a gym is walkable from a station.
  4. Price the whole first year. Add joining fees, any required starter package, taxes and add-ons to 12 months of dues before comparing. The $298 Foundation package at CrossFit Midtown, for example, adds about $25 a month to the first year.
  5. Use the trial at the hour you'd train. CrossFit Buckhead's free intro, Urban Body Fitness's $25 week and Atlanta Barbell's refundable day pass all let you see how busy the floor is. If you've been away a while, our guide to returning to the gym after a long break has a sensible first-month plan.
  6. Read the cancellation terms before you sign. We couldn't verify Georgia's health-club contract rules for this guide, so your contract's own terms are what you have. Check the notice period and how you have to cancel, and keep a copy. For federal rules, see our guide to gym fees and how cancellation works.

How we chose these gyms

We built this list from desk research, not visits. We didn't tour, train at or get paid by any of these gyms, and none paid to be included. We started from gyms with an official website showing current 2026 details, such as published hours, live booking or pricing pages, or dated posts. We kept only gyms we could cross-check against at least one independent listing at the same address. We dropped any we couldn't confirm as open, including one that announced its closure in 2025. We aimed for a spread of budgets and training styles: a premium athletic club, an independent full-service gym, a dedicated strength gym, two CrossFit boxes, a nonprofit YMCA and a 24/7 access club. Prices, hours and day-pass terms come from each gym's own website. We don't use star ratings or review counts. Details were checked on 12 October 2026 and the main prices re-checked on 18 October 2026.

Some options are missing because we couldn't verify them. Planet Fitness blocked our automated checks. Crunch, Anytime Fitness and EōS location pages couldn't be read. Atlanta Women's Barbell Club, the women-focused candidate, had no dated 2026 content and a broken schedule page, so we couldn't confirm it's operating. CrossFit Atlanta announced a temporary closure in March 2025 with no reopening posted. We'll add any of them once we can confirm details from official sources.

How we score these gyms

Each gym gets a score from 1 to 5 on five criteria, in half-point steps. The overall score is the average of the five, rounded to one decimal place. We score only what each gym publishes, as described in this guide. We did not visit, tour or train at any of these gyms, and no gym paid for its score.

When a gym doesn't publish its price, its value score is capped at 3.0, because you can't judge value without a price. A specialist gym can still score highly on facilities if its equipment is strong for what it does. These scores are a starting point for your shortlist. They are not a substitute for a trial visit at the time you'd actually train.

  • Value: the price compared with what's included, after joining, annual and processing fees.
  • Facilities: the equipment and amenities listed, such as platforms, pools, saunas, recovery areas and studios.
  • Access: opening hours (24/7 scores highest), day passes or free trials, and the number of locations.
  • Coaching: the range of classes, coaching and programming on offer.
  • Transparency: whether prices, contract and cancellation terms, and hours are clearly published.

FAQ

What is the cheapest gym in Atlanta?

Among verified published prices, the YMCA's Young Adult rate is $49 a month for ages 18 to 29 (plus a joining fee), Urban Body Fitness is $62.99 a month, and Atlanta Barbell is $90 a month with no contract. National budget chains such as Planet Fitness may cost less, but we couldn't verify their Atlanta clubs for this guide.

Are there 24-hour gyms in Atlanta?

Workout Anytime gives members 24/7 access at its Howell Mill Road club, with other clubs in Cascade and Decatur. Life Time Buckhead opens 4am to midnight on weekdays and Atlanta Barbell 5am to 11pm daily, but neither is 24/7.

Can I get a gym day pass in Atlanta?

Yes. Atlanta Barbell charges $20 a day, CrossFit Buckhead $30 a class and CrossFit Midtown $25 to $30, with experience requirements at both CrossFit gyms. Life Time and the YMCA sell day passes without publishing the price. Urban Body Fitness offers a $25 seven-day trial instead.

How much is Life Time in Atlanta?

Buckhead adult membership starts at $299 a month and North Druid Hills at $369, both month-to-month with 30 days' notice.

What is the best gym in Atlanta for strength training?

Atlanta Barbell is the dedicated free-weight gym on this list, with coached programming, $90 month-to-month membership and 5am–11pm hours. CrossFit Midtown's Barbell Club add-on covers Olympic weightlifting.

Do Atlanta CrossFit gyms take drop-ins?

Yes. CrossFit Buckhead charges $30 a class or $90 a week and asks for three months of experience. CrossFit Midtown charges $30, or $25 in advance, and asks for a year of experience or a completed CrossFit Open.

Is there a women-only gym in Atlanta?

We couldn't verify one for this guide. Atlanta Women's Barbell Club in Decatur was the obvious candidate, but its site had no dated 2026 content when we checked. Atlanta Barbell states an inclusive mission and writes individual programs, but it isn't a women's gym.
