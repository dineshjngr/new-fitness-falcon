The best gym in Dallas depends on your budget, your hours and which side of town you live on. For the lowest verified monthly price, Crunch Fitness Central Forest starts at $9.99 a month with an annual commitment. For three city-centre clubs on one card, Trophy Fitness covers Uptown, Downtown and Mockingbird for $109 a month. For a premium club, Life Time Dallas – Highland Park starts at $279. For 24/7 entry, PARADIGM Gym in Lakewood is the one we could verify. The Moody Family YMCA has the only lap pool on this list, and CrossFit Odyssey is the pick for coached classes and HYROX training.

Key takeaways

  • Crunch Fitness Central Forest is the cheapest verified gym in Dallas at $9.99 a month with an annual commitment, or $15.99 without, but the club page doesn't show its fees.
  • Trophy Fitness gives you three central Dallas clubs (Uptown, Downtown, Mockingbird) for $109 a month, with saunas, classes and free parking, but no visitor day pass.
  • PARADIGM Gym in Lakewood is the only verified 24/7 gym on the list, at $169 a month with no enrollment fee; Life Time Highland Park starts at $279 and opens at 4:30am.
  • Texas law lets you cancel a new gym contract for a full refund within three business days by certified mail, and caps contract terms at three years.

How this list was made: The Fitness Falcon did not visit, tour or train at any of these gyms. This is a research-based guide. Every gym was checked on its official website and at least one independent listing between 12 and 18 October 2026. No gym paid to be included. Prices are exactly as each gym publishes them, and where a gym doesn't publish a price we say so. What gym memberships cost in Dallas

Among the gyms we could verify, published monthly prices run from $9.99 (Crunch, with an annual commitment) to $279 (Life Time). The headline number is only part of the cost:

  • Commitment terms. Crunch's cheapest rate needs a 12-month commitment; its no-commitment rate is $15.99. CrossFit Midtown-style 12-month contracts don't appear on this list, but always ask.
  • Enrollment and annual fees. Crunch doesn't show them on its club page, Trophy doesn't list them, and Paradigm advertises no enrollment fee on its $169 plan.
  • Notice periods. Life Time asks for 30 days' notice. Others don't publish their cancellation terms, so get them in writing.
  • Resident-only offers. Trophy's 7-day trial and Crunch's free first day both need local ID. Visitors can pay $15 at Crunch, $20 at the Y, $30 at Paradigm or CrossFit Odyssey, or $40 at Diesel.

Planet Fitness, the usual answer to "cheapest gym," has several Dallas clubs, including one in East Dallas that an independent listing shows as 24 hours. Its website blocked our automated checks, so it isn't listed here. Our US gym chain price comparison covers Planet Fitness and its annual fee from the company's own filings.

How to choose a gym in Dallas

  1. Start with your schedule. Only PARADIGM on this list is open 24/7. Life Time opens earliest among the rest, at 4:30am on weekdays. Class gyms like CrossFit Odyssey only work if the class times fit your day.
  2. Pick a side of town. The picks here cover Uptown and CityPlace (Trophy, Diesel), the North Central Expressway corridor (Life Time, Crunch Central Forest), Lakewood (Paradigm), Bluffview (CrossFit Odyssey) and Preston Road (Moody Y). Dallas is spread out and most people drive, so a gym on your commute beats a better one across town. That's general advice, not a measured finding.
  3. Ask about parking. Trophy Uptown publishes free street and two-hour garage parking. Paradigm Lakewood and CrossFit Odyssey have private lots according to independent listings. Nobody else on this list publishes parking details.
  4. Price the whole first year. Add any enrollment or annual fee, taxes and add-ons to 12 months of dues before you compare. The Crunch and Trophy pages don't show every fee, so ask.
  5. Use the trial at the time you'd train. The Y's 3-day trial, Trophy's 7-day trial and Crunch's free day all let you see how busy the floor is at your hour. If you've been away a while, our guide to returning to the gym after a long break has a sensible first-month plan.
  6. Read the cancellation terms before you sign. Check the notice period and how you have to cancel. Texas law adds a few protections, below.

Texas gym contract basics

Texas regulates gym and health club contracts under the Health Spa Act, Chapter 702 of the Texas Occupations Code. The same rules apply in Dallas as in our Houston gym guide. The main points for members:

  • Three-business-day cancellation. You can cancel a new contract for a full refund if you send written notice by midnight of the third business day after the contract date, by certified mail to the gym's home office, with proof of payment. The refund is due within 30 days (§702.307).
  • Maximum term. A contract can't run longer than three years. If it's financed through a retail installment contract, payments can't run longer than five years (§702.303).
  • Other cancellation rights. You may cancel and get a refund of unearned payments if the gym closes without providing another facility within 10 miles, moves more than 10 miles away, fails to provide the services it advertised, or if the member dies or becomes totally and permanently disabled (§702.308).
  • Moving away isn't on the list. Texas doesn't give you a statutory right to cancel because you relocate. That depends on your contract.

Most of the gyms here sell month-to-month memberships with their own notice periods, which are separate from these statutory rights. Keep a copy of your contract and any cancellation letter. For federal rules and other states, see our guide to gym fees and how cancellation works.

This is general information, not legal advice. For a specific dispute, read your contract and contact the Texas Attorney General's consumer protection division or a lawyer.

How we score these gyms

Each gym gets a score from 1 to 5 on five criteria, in half-point steps. The overall score is the average of the five, rounded to one decimal place. We score only what each gym publishes, as described in this guide. We did not visit, tour or train at any of these gyms, and no gym paid for its score.

When a gym doesn't publish its price, its value score is capped at 3.0, because you can't judge value without a price. A specialist gym can still score highly on facilities if its equipment is strong for what it does. These scores are a starting point for your shortlist. They are not a substitute for a trial visit at the time you'd actually train.

  • Value: the price compared with what's included, after enrollment, annual and processing fees.
  • Facilities: the equipment and amenities listed, such as platforms, pools, saunas, recovery areas and studios.
  • Access: opening hours (24/7 scores highest), day passes or free trials, and the number of locations.
  • Coaching: the range of classes, coaching and programming on offer.
  • Transparency: whether prices, contract and cancellation terms, and hours are clearly published.

FAQ

What is the cheapest gym in Dallas?

Of the gyms we verified, Crunch Fitness Central Forest is the cheapest at $9.99 a month with an annual commitment, or $15.99 with none, plus fees that aren't shown on the club page. Planet Fitness may be comparable but blocked our checks.

Are there 24-hour gyms in Dallas?

Yes. PARADIGM Gym 24hr gives members app-based entry around the clock at its Lakewood and Lemmon Ave locations. Crunch Central Forest and Life Time Highland Park are not 24/7.

Can I buy a gym day pass in Dallas?

Yes. The YMCA charges $20, Crunch $15 for non-locals, Paradigm $30, CrossFit Odyssey $30 for experienced drop-ins, and Diesel $40 for out-of-town guests. Life Time's One Day membership runs $30 to $100 depending on the club.

Which Dallas gyms offer a free trial?

The YMCA offers a 3-day trial, Trophy a 7-day trial for Dallas residents, Crunch a free first day for locals, and CrossFit Odyssey a free fitness assessment and a free HYROX trial class.

How much is Life Time in Dallas?

Adult membership at Life Time Dallas – Highland Park starts at $279 a month, month-to-month with 30 days' notice. Life Time says prices vary by location, so confirm with the club.

Is there a powerlifting gym in Dallas?

Progressive Overload Dallas, just north of the city in Richardson, publishes a competition-style equipment list (Eleiko and Rogue calibrated plates, IPF-style combo racks, specialty bars) with membership from $75 a month on a six-month contract, and an independent listing shows 24-hour access. We left it off the main list because its website had an invalid security certificate when we checked, so you may see a browser warning. If you lift seriously, call before you go, and read our guide to progressive overload for beginners if you're still building the basics.

Which Dallas gym has a pool?

The Moody Family YMCA has a six-lane lap pool plus an instructional pool. None of the other gyms on this list publishes a pool.

Can I cancel a gym contract in Texas?

Yes, within three business days of signing, by certified mail, for a full refund. You can also cancel later if the gym closes or moves more than 10 miles, or fails to provide advertised services. After that, your contract's own terms apply.
