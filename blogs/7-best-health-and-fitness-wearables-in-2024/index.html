7 Best Health and Fitness Wearables in 2024

By Dinesh September 30, 2026 11 min read 0 Comments

Health and fitness wearables have become essential tools for individuals looking to track and improve their well-being. These devices provide valuable insights into our daily activities, exercise routines, sleep patterns, and overall health. In 2024, the market is flooded with a wide range of options, making it challenging to choose the best health and fitness wearable that suits your needs. In this article, we will explore the top health and fitness wearables in 2024. These devices offer advanced features and functionalities that go beyond basic activity tracking. Whether you're a fitness enthusiast or someone who wants to maintain a healthy lifestyle, these wearables can help you achieve your goals. So, let's dive into the world of healthcare gadgets and discover the best options available in 2024. From budget-friendly fitness trackers to premium smartwatches, we've got you covered. Get ready to explore the features and benefits of these wearables that can revolutionize your health and fitness journey. Let's explore the world of health and fitness wearables together!

1. Fitbit Inspire 3

Fitbit Inspire 3 is a budget-friendly health and fitness wearable that offers advanced tracking features for users looking to monitor their overall well-being. Here is an overview of Fitbit Inspire 3, its unique features, and the benefits it provides:

Overview

Fitbit Inspire 3 is positioned in the market as an affordable option for individuals who want a comprehensive health monitoring device without breaking the bank. It caters to both fitness enthusiasts and beginners who are looking to track their progress and improve their lifestyle.

Key Features

What sets Fitbit Inspire 3 apart from other wearables is its inclusion of sleep stage tracking and guided breathing exercises. Here are the key features of Fitbit Inspire 3:
  1. Sleep Stage Tracking: Monitor your sleep patterns, including the duration and quality of your sleep.
  2. Guided Breathing Exercises: Manage stress levels with personalized breathing sessions that promote relaxation and mindfulness.

Benefits

Fitbit Inspire 3 offers several benefits that can enhance your health and fitness journey:
  • Real-time heart rate monitoring during workouts helps you stay within your target heart rate zones for optimal training results.
  • Personalized activity goals based on your fitness level encourage you to reach your targets and maintain an active lifestyle.
Overall, Fitbit Inspire 3 is a cost-effective option that combines affordability with advanced health and fitness tracking capabilities. Its sleep stage tracking, guided breathing exercises, real-time heart rate monitoring, and personalized activity goals make it a valuable tool for individuals who want to improve their overall well-being. Whether you're a beginner or a seasoned fitness enthusiast, Fitbit Inspire 3 has something to offer in your health journey.

2. Fitbit Charge 6

Fitbit Charge 6 is a popular wearable device known for its sleek design and advanced features that cater to fitness enthusiasts. Here are some key features and specifications that make it stand out:
  • Haptic side button: The device has a haptic side button that allows for easy navigation, making it more convenient to use.
  • Built-in GPS: With built-in GPS, the Fitbit Charge 6 can accurately track outdoor activities, giving users precise information about their workouts and movements.
  • Continuous heart rate monitoring: This device can continuously monitor your heart rate, letting you keep an eye on your heart health during different activities and throughout the day.
  • Google Fit integration: Users can connect their Fitbit Charge 6 with Google Fit, which opens up access to a wide range of apps and services, expanding what the device can do.
  • Multi-day battery life: One of the standout features of this device is its long battery life, which means you won't have to charge it as often and can use it without interruptions.
  • ECG readings: For more comprehensive health tracking, the Fitbit Charge 6 can provide ECG readings, giving you valuable insights into your heart's activity.
By using these features, you can make the most of your health and fitness tracking experience with the Fitbit Charge 6.

3. Fitbit Sense 2

Fitbit Sense 2 is a top-notch smartwatch that offers advanced health and wellness features. It is designed to give users detailed information about their overall well-being. Here are the important points about Fitbit Sense 2:

Key Features of Fitbit Sense 2

  • Skin Temperature Sensor: Fitbit Sense 2 can measure skin temperature, which can indicate changes in body temperature or signs of illness.
  • ECG Readings: This smartwatch has an app that can assess heart health by measuring the electrical activity of the heart.
  • Stress Tracking: Fitbit Sense 2 includes tools for managing stress levels and provides insights based on heart rate variability data.
  • Sleep Monitoring: With advanced sleep tracking capabilities, Fitbit Sense 2 provides detailed information about sleep quality, duration, and stages.

Benefits of Using Fitbit Sense 2 for Health and Fitness Tracking

Using Fitbit Sense 2 for health and fitness tracking offers several benefits:
  • Stay proactive about health: By monitoring skin temperature, users can keep an eye out for any potential health issues and seek medical advice if needed.
  • Detect heart issues early: ECG readings provide valuable information about heart rhythm, helping users identify any irregularities at an early stage.
  • Manage stress effectively: The stress tracking feature helps users understand their stress levels and provides guided breathing exercises to promote relaxation.
  • Improve sleep quality: Comprehensive sleep monitoring allows users to identify factors affecting their sleep and make necessary changes for better rest.
With its combination of style and advanced health features, Fitbit Sense 2 is a great choice for those who want a fashionable smartwatch with powerful health tracking capabilities. Whether you're focused on improving your fitness or simply want to stay informed about your well-being, this smartwatch has you covered.

4. Garmin Venu Sq 2

The Garmin Venu Sq 2 is a great option for people who want both style and functionality in a smartwatch. Here are some important things to know about it:

Overview

The Garmin Venu Sq 2 is perfect for those who want a stylish and functional wearable device. It's popular among fitness enthusiasts because of its modern design and advanced features.

Key Features

Here are some key features of the Garmin Venu Sq 2:
  • Extended battery life: You can use the Garmin Venu Sq 2 for up to 10 days without charging it, which is very convenient.
  • Many sports apps: It comes with a lot of preloaded apps for different sports, so you can easily track your workouts no matter what activity you're into.

Benefits

Here's how the Garmin Venu Sq 2 can benefit you:
  • For health and fitness: With the Garmin Venu Sq 2, you can control your music from your phone using the watch. This is great because you don't have to stop your workout just to change songs or adjust the volume on your phone.
All these reasons make the Garmin Venu Sq 2 a great choice for anyone who wants a versatile and reliable wearable device for their health and fitness needs.

5. Garmin Venu 2

Overview of Garmin Venu 2

The Garmin Venu 2 stands out with its unique squarish display design, which offers improved text legibility, especially in outdoor environments. This design choice enhances the user experience by ensuring better visibility and readability during various activities.

Key Features and Specifications

One of the key highlights of the Garmin Venu 2 is its impressive battery life, capable of supporting up to 9 days of typical use on a single charge. This extended battery life ensures that users can rely on the device for an extended period without frequent recharging. Additionally, the built-in GPS feature enables accurate distance tracking during outdoor workouts, catering to the needs of users who engage in activities such as running, cycling, or hiking.
  • Squarish display: Enhances text legibility in outdoor environments.
  • Impressive battery life: Supports up to 9 days of typical use.
  • Built-in GPS: Allows accurate distance tracking during outdoor workouts.

6. Garmin Venu 2 Plus

The Garmin Venu 2 Plus is the ultimate smartwatch for tech-savvy individuals who want a seamless connected experience on their wrist. It offers a range of features that make it a true powerhouse in the world of health and fitness wearables:
  • Music Streaming/Control via Phone: With the Garmin Venu 2 Plus, you can stream music directly from your phone to your watch. You no longer need to carry your phone during workouts to enjoy your favorite tunes. The watch allows you to control playback and adjust volume, all from the convenience of your wrist.
  • Local Music Storage/Playback: If you prefer to leave your phone behind, the Garmin Venu 2 Plus offers local music storage. You can transfer your favorite songs directly to the watch and listen to them using Bluetooth headphones. This feature is perfect for those who want to enjoy their music without relying on a smartphone.
  • Calendar Access: Stay organized and never miss an important event with calendar access on your Garmin Venu 2 Plus. You can view upcoming appointments, meetings, and reminders right on your wrist. This feature ensures that you stay on top of your schedule while focusing on your health and fitness goals.
  • Smart Notifications: Receive notifications for calls, messages, emails, and social media alerts directly on your Garmin Venu 2 Plus. Stay connected without constantly checking your phone. The watch vibrates and displays a preview of incoming notifications, allowing you to decide whether they require immediate attention or can be dealt with later.
  • Garmin Pay: The Garmin Venu 2 Plus offers contactless payments through Garmin Pay. Leave your wallet at home and make purchases conveniently using just your watch. This feature is not only convenient but also adds an extra layer of security by eliminating the need to carry credit cards or cash during workouts or daily activities.
  • Hands-Free Calling: Pair your smartphone with the Garmin Venu 2 Plus and enjoy the convenience of hands-free calling. You can answer or reject calls directly from your watch, allowing you to keep your phone safely tucked away while staying connected on the go.
The Garmin Venu 2 Plus provides all these features alongside its advanced health and fitness tracking capabilities. It's a smartwatch that offers not only connectivity but also comprehensive monitoring and analysis of your well-being, making it an excellent choice for those who want a fully integrated wearable device.

Other Notable Options

In addition to the top picks, there are several other health and fitness wearables in 2024 that cater to specific needs and preferences. While they may not have made it to the top list, these options offer unique features and functionalities that make them noteworthy choices for certain users:
  1. Garmin Vivomove Trend - Combining the classic look of an analog watch with essential smart features, this hybrid smartwatch tracks your steps, monitors sleep, and even includes a relaxation timer to help manage stress.
  2. Garmin Vivomove Sport - Another hybrid smartwatch from Garmin, the Vivomove Sport offers wellness tracking tools while maintaining a sporty design. It provides advanced fitness metrics and long battery life.
  3. Oura Ring - This sleek and lightweight ring wearable focuses on sleep tracking, providing insights into sleep stages, recovery, and overall sleep quality. It also offers activity tracking and readiness scores to optimize performance.
  4. Garmin Forerunner 265 and Forerunner 255 - Specifically designed for running enthusiasts, these GPS running watches offer advanced training features, performance monitoring tools, and personalized coaching to improve running efficiency.
These options may not have the same level of recognition as the top wearables, but they excel in specific areas, making them valuable alternatives for individuals with distinct health and fitness priorities.

Conclusion

As you start your fitness journey, it's important to choose the right health and fitness wearable that will help you track and improve your well-being. The best health and fitness wearables in 2024 have a wide range of features to keep you motivated and help you achieve your fitness goals. Here are some key things to keep in mind:
  1. Consider your needs: Each recommended wearable has its own features and specifications. To make the best choice, it's important to read detailed reviews of each device and choose one that fits what you're looking for.
  2. Think about what matters most: Finding the right balance between features, design, and cost is important. Think about what's most important to you - whether it's advanced health tracking, a stylish design, or affordability.
  3. Look for comprehensive health tracking: The best wearables have features like heart rate monitoring, sleep tracking, stress management tools, and GPS for accurate activity tracking. Look for a device that covers all aspects of your fitness journey.
  4. Personalize your device: Many wearables come in different styles and colors, so you can choose one that suits your preferences.
Remember that health and fitness wearables are tools to help you on your fitness journey. They give you feedback in real-time, help you set goals, and track your activities to keep you motivated. So take some time to do research and find the perfect companion for your fitness goals. With the right wearable device, you'll be able to see how far you've come and make positive changes towards a healthier lifestyle. Happy tracking!
