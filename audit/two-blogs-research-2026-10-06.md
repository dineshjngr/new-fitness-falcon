# Two new articles — research and editorial record

## Topic and competing-page review

Checked on 6 October 2026. This is a qualitative search-intent review, not a paid keyword-volume study or a claim to have reviewed every competing page.

### Beginner dumbbell workout at home

- [SELF's beginner dumbbell workout](https://www.self.com/gallery/beginner-dumbbell-workout) provides trainer attribution, a compact four-movement session, and demonstrations. It is a useful benchmark for explaining technique and adapting exercises.
- [StrengthLog's beginner programme](https://www.strengthlog.com/beginner-dumbbell-program/) presents alternating workouts, explicit sets/repetitions, progression, and a tracker. This is a useful benchmark for repeatable scheduling and recording progress.
- Our angle: an introductory no-bench session with unweighted learning options, a clear explanation of what “per side” means, a small progression decision table, and an equipment-neutral log. The schedule is labelled as our illustrative assembly rather than a clinically tested or professionally reviewed programme. Health/training claims rely on primary organisations, not competing publishers.

### Increasing fibre in Indian meals

- [What2Eat's Indian food collection](https://www.what2eat.in/nutrition/collections/high-fiber-indian-foods) offers many dishes and per-100-g values. Our practical angle adds guidance about dry versus cooked weights, recipe variation, actual serving size, and a worked hypothetical label calculation.
- [The NHS fibre guide](https://www.nhs.uk/live-well/eat-well/digestive-health/how-to-get-more-fibre-into-your-diet/) gives clear food-group advice and contextual UK targets. We localise the meal examples while explaining that UK guidance is not an individual Indian prescription.
- Also reviewed search summaries of Indian food-ranking and day-of-eating guides. Some pages could not be retrieved in full; no detailed claims about their coverage are made here.
- Our angle: ordinary poha, dal/rice, roti, and sambar options; one change at a time; a flexible starter sequence; no invented exact fibre totals for variable home recipes; appropriate limits for ongoing symptoms.

## Source basis

- Workout: CDC adult activity recommendations; ACSM's 2026 guidance; Mayo Clinic technique guidance; ACE goblet squat, single-arm row, and Romanian deadlift instructions; NHS strength exercises; NASM dead bug and floor bridge demonstrations.
- Fibre: NHS fibre guidance, BDA fibre fact sheet, NIDDK diet guidance, and NIDDK constipation symptoms/causes.
- Each source is linked next to relevant claims and collected at the end of the article. Exact URLs are also stored in each post's `sources` field and BlogPosting citations.
