## Evidence used in the articles

### Walking-pad tracking

- [Apple measurements](https://support.apple.com/en-us/105002): movement, workout selection, wrist fit, and exercise credit. Recording a workout is distinguished from restoring missing steps.
- [Apple calibration](https://support.apple.com/en-us/105048): supported outdoor procedure and distance/pace/calorie scope. No promise that calibration fixes a stationary wrist.
- [Apple Health data](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108779): Fitness Tracking permissions and data-source prioritization. Phone and watch totals must not simply be added together.
- [Garmin step troubleshooting](https://support.garmin.com/en-US/?faq=z1TfJCqajl8ZEZey72gg98): limited arm movement and inability to edit step data in Garmin Connect.
- [Garmin treadmill activity](https://support.garmin.com/en-ZA/?faq=bpO2rljrqH2yUG1wSG73s9): arm movement and indoor distance limitations. Model-specific calibration instructions are not generalized.
- [Samsung step troubleshooting](https://www.samsung.com/us/support/troubleshoot/TSG10004732/): restricted hand movement can affect step detection.

The stationary-wrist explanation is explicitly an inference from manufacturer guidance. The short comparison test and log are editorial suggestions. No first-person testing, device accuracy percentage, or manufacturer endorsement is invented.

### Post-meal walking

- [Reynolds et al., 2016](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00125-016-4085-2): 41 adults with type 2 diabetes, two-week crossover periods, daily walking advice versus 10 minutes after main meals. The 12% and 22% differences refer to incremental postprandial glucose exposure, not guaranteed individual readings or weight loss.
- [Engeroff et al., 2023](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s40279-022-01808-7): acute post-meal response findings and high overall risk of bias in included studies.
- [Slebe et al., 2024](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.diabres.2024.111638): 28 intervention studies, no clear overall timing advantage in the broader analyses, heterogeneous protocols and limited evidence quality. The university-hosted [author paper](https://dspace.library.uu.nl/bitstream/handle/1874/455024/PIIS0168822724001281.pdf?sequence=1) was also located.
- [Babir et al., 2026](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00125-026-06741-2): vigorous bodyweight exercise snacks in 31 adults, not gentle walks. Primary mean-glucose outcome did not reach statistical significance; secondary improvements are not generalized to the beginner plan.
- [CDC adult guidance](https://www.cdc.gov/physical-activity-basics/guidelines/adults.html) and [intensity guidance](https://www.cdc.gov/physical-activity-basics/measuring/index.html): weekly activity targets, shorter bouts, and the talk test.
- [ADA glucose and exercise](https://diabetes.org/health-wellness/fitness/blood-glucose-and-exercise): glucose response and medication-related low-glucose risk. No dosing or individualized glucose-threshold instructions are supplied.

The seven-day starting plan is labeled editorial guidance rather than a validated treatment. The article separates short-term glucose outcomes, full-day glucose, long-term health, and weight loss.
